Homework · Week 9.1

Lighthouse Accessibility Audit

Check the accessibility of your personal or team project, fix problems found by Lighthouse, and share evidence of your improvements.

Your task

  1. Open your personal or team project site in Chrome. Open Developer Tools, choose the Lighthouse panel, and run an audit with only the Accessibility category selected.
  2. Save a screenshot of the initial Accessibility score and audit results before making changes.
  3. Fix at least three failing accessibility audits from the report. Make the fixes in your project, then check that each issue is resolved.
  4. Run the same Lighthouse Accessibility audit again and save a screenshot of the improved result.
  5. Post both screenshots and a list describing your three or more fixes on the weekly task page.

Goal: Aim for a Lighthouse Accessibility score of 100 after your fixes. Your initial screenshot should show the score before the changes.

Submission checklist

  • Initial Lighthouse Accessibility screenshot (before fixes)
  • Improved Lighthouse Accessibility screenshot (after fixes)
  • A list of at least three accessibility issues you fixed
  • Both screenshots and the fix list posted to the weekly task page
