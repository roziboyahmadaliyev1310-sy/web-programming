How to Prepare Korean Kimchi Ramen

What You Need

  • 1 packet of instant ramen
  • 500ml water
  • 1/2 cup aged kimchi (sliced)
  • 1 green onion (chopped)
  • 1 fresh egg

Steps

  1. Bring 500ml of water to a roll boil in a small pot.
  2. Add the sliced kimchi and broth powder; simmer for 2 minutes to build the flavor base.
  3. Drop in the ramen noodles and cook for exactly 3 minutes.
  4. Warning: Crack the egg into the boiling soup without stirring if you prefer a intact yolk.
  5. Garnish with fresh green onions and serve hot immediately.
