The History of the World Wide Web

Published by Wikimedia Foundation · September 5, 2026

In 1989, Tim Berners-Lee proposed an information management system that eventually evolved into the World Wide Web. It allowed document sharing over the global Internet using hyperlinked markup files.

Quick Fact

The world's first website was hosted on Tim Berners-Lee's NeXT computer at CERN in 1991.

Today, the Web standard is maintained by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), ensuring interoperability across browsers worldwide.
